# Inland Marine Equipment Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5009
Effective: 2025-02-15

COVERED PROPERTY
Scheduled mobile equipment including cranes, forklifts, and portable generators are covered while in transit or at temporary job sites.
VALUATION
Replacement cost applies for owned equipment; actual cash value for rented equipment.
DEDUCTIBLE
$1,000 per occurrence for theft away from a secured yard.
EXCLUSIONS
Wear, mechanical breakdown, and freezing of fluid systems without winterization are excluded.
