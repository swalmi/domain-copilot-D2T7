# Commercial Cyber Liability Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5002
Effective: 2025-03-01

COVERED EVENTS
We cover losses arising from a cybersecurity incident including data breach response costs, business interruption from system failure, and extortion payments subject to prior insurer consent.
BREACH RESPONSE
Forensic investigation, notification, credit monitoring, and public relations costs are covered up to $250,000 per claim.
BUSINESS INTERRUPTION
Waiting period is 12 hours. Daily benefit max is $10,000 for up to 30 days.
EXCLUSIONS
Prior known incidents, infrastructure failure of unencrypted backups, and intentional acts by the insured are excluded.
