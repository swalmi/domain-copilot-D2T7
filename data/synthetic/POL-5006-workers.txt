# Workers Compensation Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5006
Effective: 2025-01-15

COVERAGE
We provide statutory workers compensation benefits for accidental injury arising out of and in the course of employment.
EMPLOYERS LIABILITY
Employers liability limits are $100,000/$500,000/$100,000 for bodily injury by accident, bodily injury by disease, and each employee.
EXCLUSIONS
Intentional self-inflicted injury, commuting accidents outside employer control, and independent contractor injuries are excluded.
REPORTING
Injuries must be reported to the carrier within 24 hours of employer knowledge.
