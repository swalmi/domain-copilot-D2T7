# Pet Health Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5004
Effective: 2025-02-01

ACCIDENT AND ILLNESS
We cover eligible accident and illness veterinary expenses after the waiting period: 14 days for accidents and 30 days for illness.
ANNUAL LIMIT
The annual benefit limit is $10,000 per insured pet.
DEDUCTIBLE
Choose $100, $250, or $500 annual deductible; reimbursement is 70% of eligible expenses after deductible.
EXCLUSIONS
Pre-existing conditions, breeding, cosmetics, and routine wellness exams are excluded unless a wellness rider is purchased.
