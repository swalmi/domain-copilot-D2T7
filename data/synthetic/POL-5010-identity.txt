# Personal Identity Theft Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5010
Effective: 2025-07-01

COVERED EXPENSES
We cover costs to restore identity including legal fees, lost wages up to $1,500 per week for 8 weeks, and re-issuance of documents.
MONITORING
Credit monitoring for 12 months is included after a confirmed identity theft event.
LIMIT
Aggregate limit is $25,000 per policy period.
EXCLUSIONS
Funds transferred with voluntary consent of the insured and losses known prior to coverage inception are excluded.
