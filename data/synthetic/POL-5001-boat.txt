# Boat and Marine Liability Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5001
Effective: 2025-01-01

SECTION I - PERILS INSURED
We cover direct physical loss or damage to the insured watercraft caused by fire, lightning, explosion, theft, vandalism, and collision while navigating.
SECTION II - DEDUCTIBLE
The deductible for each occurrence is $1,000 for hull damage and $2,500 for liability claims.
SECTION III - LIMITS
The maximum policy limit for hull coverage is $75,000. Liability limit per occurrence is $300,000.
EXCLUSIONS
Flood, tsunami, wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, and war risks are excluded.
ADDITIONAL COVERAGES
Emergency towing up to $500 per disablement is included when the vessel is stranded.
