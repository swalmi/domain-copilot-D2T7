# Flood Endorsement Sample

Policy ID: POL-5005
Effective: 2024-11-01

COVERED FLOOD TYPES
This endorsement covers flood as defined by rising water, overflow of rivers or streams, tidal wave, and storm surge caused by named storms.
BUILDING AND CONTENTS
Building limit $250,000; contents limit $100,000. Deductible is 2% of coverage A, minimum $2,000.
EXCLUSIONS
Groundwater seepage, mudflow not associated with floodwater, and earth movement are excluded.
CLAIMS
Proof of loss must be filed within 60 days of loss discovery.
