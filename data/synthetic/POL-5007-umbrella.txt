# Commercial Umbrella Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5007
Effective: 2025-04-01

ATTACHED POLICIES
This umbrella follows form over scheduled underlying general auto and employers liability policies.
LIMIT
The umbrella limit is $1,000,000 each occurrence and aggregate, excess of $1,000,000 underlying.
SELF-INSURED RETENTION
A self-insured retention of $10,000 applies where underlying coverage is exhausted or unavailable.
EXCLUSIONS
Contractual liability assumed without insurable interest, pollution on owned sites, and professional errors are excluded.
