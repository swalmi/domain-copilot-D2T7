# Travel Medical Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5003
Effective: 2025-06-15

ELIGIBILITY
Coverage applies to insured persons while traveling outside their country of residence for trips up to 180 days.
MEDICAL BENEFITS
Emergency medical treatment, hospitalization, and ambulance services are covered up to $1,000,000 per trip.
DEDUCTIBLE
A $250 deductible applies per calendar year for non-emergency outpatient care.
EXCLUSIONS
Pre-existing conditions not stabilized for 180 days, elective cosmetic procedures, and participation in professional sports are excluded.
EVIACUATION
Medical evacuation benefit is $250,000 and requires insurer authorization before transport when safe to do so.
