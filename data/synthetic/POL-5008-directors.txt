# Directors and Officers Sample Policy

Policy ID: POL-5008
Effective: 2025-05-01

CLAIMS MADE
Coverage is claims-made; the retroactive date is the policy inception date and the extended reporting period is 60 days.
DEFENSE
Defense costs are outside the limit when insurer counsel is used with consent.
LIMIT
Limit of liability is $5,000,000 aggregate.
EXCLUSIONS
Fraud committed with final adjudication, bodily injury, and knowing violation of law are excluded.
